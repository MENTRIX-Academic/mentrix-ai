MENTRIX Sprint 07 — Handwriting reading, simple steps

1. Extract this ZIP and open the MENTRIX folder.
2. Close the old MENTRIX terminal (Ctrl+C).
3. Double-click START_MENTRIX_HERE.bat.
4. Double-click INSTALL_SPRINT06_OCR.bat to install/update TrOCR and OpenCV
   in the project's Python environment. Internet is required for downloading
   TrOCR model weights the first time. The weights are NOT in this ZIP.
5. Sign in to the local site. Go to Quiz Grading, upload a photo/PDF,
   open the paper, select Smart line detection, then Read handwriting with TrOCR.
6. Open Compare original handwriting with OCR to inspect each line crop.
7. If question matching fails, expand Mark question areas directly on image.
   Pick Q1, drag a rectangle around the first answer; repeat for Q2, etc.
   Press Read handwriting with TrOCR again to apply these regions.
8. Extra pass for blank/repetitive lines is optional and slower on CPU.
9. Verify each word against the paper, then Verify & grade; review unclear
   grades and use batch publish only when they are finalized.
